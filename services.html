Home/Services

Engineering, design and
privacy — under one roof.

We help founders and product organisations ship minimalist, privacy-respecting digital products. Every engagement pairs deep technical R&D with meticulous craft in interface and motion design.

Programmatic Audio Synthesis

Custom DSP pipelines, real-time synthesis and offline generative music engines. We build tools that compose unique sonic landscapes without ever uploading a single sample.

Visual Interaction Design

Motion-first interaction systems, generative visual layers and tactile feedback design. We translate complex behaviour into calm, focused interfaces.

Privacy & Security Architecture

Local-first data vaults, hardware-backed key management, on-device encryption and zero-knowledge sync. We engineer privacy into the stack, not as a layer.

Native Mobile Engineering

iOS and Android applications built with native frameworks, careful profiling and hand-tuned performance budgets. Long-term maintainability over trend-chasing.

Product Innovation Sprints

Short, focused engagements to validate product direction — from concept prototypes to user-testable MVPs. We help teams find product-market fit with clarity.

App Store Publishing

Localisation, ASO, store-compliance and lifecycle operations. Our portfolio ships to more than fifty markets with privacy-respecting monetisation stacks.

Engagement model

Lean, transparent, deeply technical.

We embed small senior teams alongside our clients — no account managers, no junior hand-offs. Every person touching your codebase has shipped production software to real users.
